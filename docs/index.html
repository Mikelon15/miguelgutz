Taking Care of Bun ​

Food ​

Bun free eats so you just have to keep the bowl of food filled up. He mainly eats at night before going to bed.

At night, add a little wet food to his regular food in order to get him to eat and you can skip having the bowl of food in the room.

Bathroom ​

He's let out to pee in the morning at 8am. He pees before bed around 11pm. He normally poops in the park in the evenings.

If he skips park or goes early, stay in the backyard till he poops. it helps to play with him by throwing the ball.

Parks ​

Scottsdale - Chaparral Park
5401 Hayden Rd, Scottsdale, AZ 85250
15 - 20 mins away
Usually busy. Best for night or in middle of the day. Best one to come to if you have 90 mins since it's like 40 mins round trip.

Chandler - Shawnee Park
1501 W Mesquite St, Chandler, AZ 85224
6 miles (15-20 mins away)
The most chill park. Also the biggest one. Busy around sunset. Empty once it gets dark. This one tends to be faster to get to than Scottsdale.

Tempe - Papago Bark Park
1000 N College Ave, Tempe, AZ 85288
6 miles (10-15 mins away) Only busy around sunset. It's the fastest to get to, but its the smallest. Best one to go to when limited on time, but have to go around 6:30-7:30.
